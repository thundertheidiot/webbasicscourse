Pure HTML+CSS Image Carousel

The containers are display: inline-block; they each have an id. The image cards are wrapped in a link that points to the corresponding id.

The supercontainer has overflow-x: scroll; display: flex; so that the cards extend off screen, and the scroll bars are hidden. Cards get scroll-snap-align: center; so the view snaps to the card.

This might be broken on chromium browsers.
